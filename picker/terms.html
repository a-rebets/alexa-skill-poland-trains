The service

The skill is a free, open-source personal project by Artem Rebets. It reads scheduled train departures and arrivals for stations you choose, with live delays when they are available. The timetable currently covers only Poland. Contributions that add other countries are welcome at github.com/a-rebets/alexa-skill-poland-trains.
